# Courtyard scene production

The storefront uses 72 generated product scenes, one for each active product family, based on supplied product references. Emerald limewash, warm sandstone with restrained petal relief, dark green stone and woven cotton create the shared visual setting. The interpretation draws on older Indian architectural and material sensibilities without claiming historical company provenance.

The complete images are displayed without white inset panels or photographic mats. Layout preserves each image's natural aspect ratio and uses contain fitting. This prevents display distortion; it does not certify generated product geometry. Original photographs remain available in the product galleries as the authoritative reference for shape, finish, markings and construction.

`courtyard-scene-manifest.json` contains all 72 prompts, reference paths, selected source options, generated assets and pixel dimensions. Generated scenes are attached only to the referenced option. Other options retain their supplied images.

Both kettle families and their generated scenes are excluded from the published catalog and assets. Seven families have only supplied AI source references and remain clearly identified. Material and specification uncertainty is preserved.

Visual research used in the original direction: [Met Shunga overview](https://www.metmuseum.org/essays/shunga-dynasty-ca-2nd-1st-century-b-c), [Bharhut lotus fragment](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/38364), [UNESCO Sanchi](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/524).
