# Storefront preview status

Published source: [d2csteel](https://github.com/anvik-anadya-raghuvar/d2csteel). The GitHub version is a public storefront preview. The earlier owner-private Sites deployment is managed independently.

The complete website contains 72 product families, 145 source option groups, 72 generated courtyard lead scenes and 783 retained source gallery entries. Both kettle families are excluded. Image compositions keep their natural proportions. Originals remain available for checking construction, markings and geometry. Seven families lack original photographs and remain flagged and excluded from the checkout rehearsal; existing material uncertainty flags are preserved.

The website includes the homepage, collections, product pages, search and filters, proposed-name study, option galleries, zoom, persistent bag, guest checkout rehearsal, test-order confirmation and lookup, guest-order history, support drafts, help and care, and policy drafts.

Prices, stock, final specifications, business contacts, shipping and returns policies, and payment credentials remain pending, as agreed. No live orders or payments are accepted. Order information stays in the viewer's browser; no order, email or support message is transmitted to the business. No customer or local test-order records are part of this repository.

Before enabling real commerce, complete these business details, connect an order backend and payment processor, approve final policies and names, and perform end-to-end purchase and fulfillment testing.
